## Financial controls

1. Record LND authorizations separately from recognized liquid resources.
2. Record independently verified counterparty settlement or lawful conversion.
3. Track payments, restricted funds, procurement, payroll, and scholarship spending separately.
4. Release budgets only after approval and funding verification.
5. Preserve supporting contracts, valuation methodology, audit trails and reporting.
