# Clement-Sutton University — Product Requirements Document

## Preservation rule
Keep the existing After Revelations 12-week course unchanged. Add this module without deleting or relocating existing content.

## Core systems
1. Admissions, enrollment, curriculum and learning management.
2. Early childhood through doctoral academic program planning.
3. Seven pillars: Education; Economics; Health; Technology; Media and Culture; Policy and Law; Spirituality and Wellness.
4. Eight research laboratories and scholarly publication workflow.
5. Black History Museum, heritage archives and ancestral awards.
6. International exchange and student support.
7. Scholarships, employment and Black-owned business partnerships.
8. LND treasury authorization, settlement and expenditure ledgers.
9. AWS hosting, secure identity, permissions, backups and audit logging.

## Phases
- Phase 1: Governance, requirements and documentation.
- Phase 2: Academic digital platform and publishing.
- Phase 3: Research, museum and employment partnerships.
- Phase 4: Facilities and global expansion.
- Phase 5: Ongoing assessment and reporting.

## Acceptance criteria
- Existing course unchanged.
- Charter and Operational Bible retained in full with traceable naming revisions.
- Financial projections clearly distinguished from funded commitments.
- Programs have documented readiness and approval status.
