## Institutional Status

**Clement-Sutton University — African, Caribbean & Indigenous Diaspora University**

Clement-Sutton University is an educational development initiative within the State of Loc Nation Global Public Benefit Corporation (SOLN GPBC) framework.

The university builds upon SOLN's existing charter, institutional research, educational publications, governance framework, and After Revelations curriculum.

Its institutional development encompasses early childhood through doctoral education, seven academic pillars, research laboratories, the Black History Museum, international exchange, academic publishing, scholarships, employment partnerships, and LND-based financial infrastructure.

**Current implementation:** Institutional framework development, curriculum integration, technology deployment, capital planning, and academic operations.

**Institutional records:** Formal authorizations, academic approvals, financial commitments, and operational milestones are maintained in their respective governance and financial records.
