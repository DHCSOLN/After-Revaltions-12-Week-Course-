# Clement-Sutton University

African, Caribbean & Indigenous Diaspora University

## Parent Institution
State of Loc Nation Global Public Benefit Corporation
(SOLN GPBC)

## Institutional Framework
Clement-Sutton University integrates education,
research, cultural preservation, entrepreneurship,
academic publishing, and international collaboration.

## Seven Pillars
1. Education
2. Economics
3. Health
4. Technology
5. Media and Culture
6. Policy and Law
7. Spirituality and Wellness

## Implementation
- Early childhood through doctoral education
- Eight research laboratories
- Black History Museum
- African, Caribbean and Indigenous exchange
- Scholarships and employment partnerships
- LND treasury and financial reporting
- Academic archives and publications
- AWS digital infrastructure

## Preservation
Existing After Revelations course content and
institutional documents remain preserved.
